Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

Nexus Home Cleaning ("we", "us", "our") is a home and office cleaning service based in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or book our services, and how we look after it.

We can be reached by phone or WhatsApp at +234 706 455 6444.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide and improve our services:

  • Contact details: your name and phone number, and any other details you share with us.
  • Booking information: your address, property type, property size, preferred dates and any special instructions.
  • Communication records: messages and quotes exchanged with us, including via WhatsApp.
  • Usage data: basic, non-identifying information about how our website is viewed (such as pages visited), if analytics are enabled.

3. How we use your information

  • To prepare free quotes and respond to your enquiries.
  • To schedule, coordinate and deliver cleaning services.
  • To contact you about your bookings, appointments and follow-ups.
  • To improve our services, quality and customer experience.
  • To send you occasional offers, only if you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your information on the basis that it is necessary to provide the services you have requested (performance of a contract), to respond to your enquiries (legitimate interest), and to comply with any legal obligations we may have.

5. How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access and disclosure. Access to your details is limited to staff who genuinely need it to complete their work.

6. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We only share information where necessary and always on a need-to-know basis:

  • Our cleaning team: the minimum details needed to reach you and complete the job.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): when you message us, your message is processed by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Legal authorities: where we are required to do so by law.

7. How long we keep information

We keep booking and contact records for as long as is reasonably necessary to provide our services, handle any disputes, and meet legal or accounting requirements. We delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed.

8. Cookies and website usage

Our website may store small data files (cookies) to improve performance and remember preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will continue to work. The WhatsApp buttons on this site open a chat in WhatsApp and are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information where there is no valid reason for us to keep it.
  • Withdraw any consent you previously gave, such as marketing consent.
  • Complain to the appropriate data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, simply message us on WhatsApp at +234 706 455 6444.

10. Third-party links

Our website and messages may contain links to third-party services such as WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external services, so please review their policies before using them.

11. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults and business owners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.

13. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us:
