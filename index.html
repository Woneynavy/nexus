Frequently asked questions

How do I book a cleaning?

Simply tap the WhatsApp button, tell us your address, property size and preferred date, and we will send you a free quote within minutes. You can also call or WhatsApp us directly on +234 706 455 6444.

Do you bring your own equipment and products?

Yes. Our team arrives fully equipped with professional tools and eco-friendly, child- and pet-safe cleaning products. All you need to do is unlock the door.

Are your cleaners vetted and trained?

Absolutely. Every member of our team is background-checked, trained on our cleaning standards and supervised on first-time jobs to ensure quality.

Which areas in Uyo do you cover?

We currently serve homes and small offices across Uyo and surrounding areas, including Uyo Town, Itiam, Nsukara, Atiku Abasi Road, Ikot Ekpene Road and environs. Message us to confirm your location.

What if I am not happy with the cleaning?

Your satisfaction is guaranteed. If anything is not up to standard, let us know within 24 hours and we will return to fix it at no extra cost.

Do you offer discounts for regular or weekly cleaning?

Yes! Customers who book weekly or monthly packages enjoy discounted rates. Ask us on WhatsApp for our current recurring plans and save on every visit.
